Connect a New Site
Follow these steps to start showing your ads on any website.
1
Add the ad script to your site
Paste this tag just before the closing </body> tag on every page you want ads to appear.
<script src="https://markohio.github.io/ad/ad.js?v=1"></script>
2
Add ad slots where you want ads to appear
Place these divs anywhere on your page. All slots are optional — only add the ones you want. The script will automatically fill them with the correct ad for that slot.
<!-- Banner ad (top of page, full width) -->
<div data-ad="banner"></div>

<!-- Half screen ad (sidebar or mid-page) -->
<div data-ad="half"></div>

<!-- Fullscreen overlay (appears once per session) -->
<div data-ad="fullscreen"></div>
3
Add the confirm pixel for click tracking
Add this one line to every page of your destination site (the site your ads link to). It tells the system when a visitor successfully arrived after clicking an ad.
<script>
  fetch(
    "https://de-mark-9fcsiixzv-markohios-projects.vercel.app/api/confirm?cid="
    + new URLSearchParams(location.search).get("cid")
  ).catch(function(){});
</script>
4
Whitelist your site's domain
Open api/click.js in your Vercel repo and add your site's domain to the ALLOWED_ORIGINS array, then push the change.
const ALLOWED_ORIGINS = [
  "https://markohio.github.io",
  "https://your-new-site.com", // add your site here
];
After adding the script and slots, create an ad with at least one image uploaded in your dashboard. The ad will automatically appear on all connected sites within seconds of being saved.
